# Brand voice & visual rules

Account: Japan travel & cultural content. Single-image posts. Target audience: aspirational travelers, foodies, culture enthusiasts.

## Voice

- **Tone:** warm, inviting, curious. Talk *with* the reader, not *at* them.
- **Person:** second person ("you") for the hook, first person plural ("we") for shared moments. Avoid "I" — this is a brand account, not a personal one.
- **Sentence rhythm:** mix short evocative lines with one longer descriptive sentence per paragraph. No walls of text.
- **Hook:** the first line must work in isolation — it's what shows in the feed before "more". Aim for a sensory image or a question, not a hashtag pile.
- **CTA:** every caption ends with one clear, low-friction ask (comment a place, save for later, tag a travel buddy). Never beg.
- **Forbidden phrases:** "absolutely should be", "bucket list" (overused on this account), "stop the scroll", "let me know in the comments", "this is your sign", "as an AI". Anything that reads like a content-marketing template.

## Format constraints (hard)

- **Caption length:** 80–180 words. Long enough to add value, short enough to read on mobile.
- **Emojis in caption body: ≤2 total.** Hashtags don't count toward this limit.
- **Hashtags: 5–10 total.** Mix one broad (e.g. `#Japan`), one mid (`#JapaneseCulture`), one niche (`#KyotoBamboo`), one community (`#TravelDiaries`), and 1–6 contextual. No banner hashtags like `#travel #travelgram #travelphotography` strung together.
- **No hashtags in the caption body.** Put them after a `.\n.\n.\n` separator at the end.
- **No links in the caption** (Instagram doesn't render them).
- **Caption must not name a specific date** unless the post is about a dated event — captions get reused, dates make them stale.

## Visual rules

- One image per post (no carousels for now — we want clean a/b signal on captions).
- Vertical 4:5 or square 1:1. Never landscape.
- Photo should have a clear subject — no busy collages with 4+ panels (we tried this on 2026-05-23 and it diluted the message).
- Color: prefer natural light, saturated but not crunched. Avoid heavy filters.
- Avoid photos with visible faces of strangers (rights issues). Backs of heads and crowds are fine.
- No on-image text unless it's a single short headline ≤6 words.

## Topic guardrails

- Stay on Japan. Sub-themes that are fair game: food, temples & shrines, gardens, seasonal nature (sakura, momiji, snow), onsen, neighborhoods (Shimokitazawa, Yanaka), crafts, transit moments, festivals.
- Avoid: politics, dietary advice, currency speculation, anything that requires a citation.
- Don't reuse the *same sub-theme* two posts in a row. Vary across food / nature / urban / craft.

## Examples

Good hook: "There's a tea shop in Yanaka where the kettle's been singing since 1959."
Bad hook: "🌸✨ Japan is calling your name — here's why you ABSOLUTELY need to visit! 🗾"

Good CTA: "Which Tokyo neighborhood feels most like home to you?"
Bad CTA: "Drop a 🌏 in the comments if Japan is on your travel wishlist!"
