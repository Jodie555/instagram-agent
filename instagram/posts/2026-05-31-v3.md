## Caption
Have you ever wandered into a dream before the city wakes up? 🌸  

Nezu Shrine, nestled in Tokyo's Bunkyo neighborhood, is a portal to another world at sunrise. Step through the iconic torii gates into the Otome Inari Shrine azalea garden, where ancient stone paths glisten with morning dew. The soft whispers of cool air carry the faint, sweet scent of azaleas, blooming in fiery shades of pink, red, and white. These flowers have their roots (quite literally) in history—the garden has drawn visitors each spring since the Edo period, when Nezu Shrine was first built in 1706.  

As you walk beneath the verdant azalea tunnel, surrounded by a sea of blossoms and tranquility, you can almost hear the footsteps of centuries past, pilgrims marveling at this same beauty.
