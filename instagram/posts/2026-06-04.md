## Caption
In the heart of Tokyo's Yanaka neighborhood, a slice of old Edo lingers quietly. Here, at the entrance of [@YanakaArtisans](https://www.instagram.com/YanakaArtisans), a carpenter’s hands tell centuries of craft. The sound of a wooden chisel meeting Japanese cypress is soft yet deliberate, resonating with the authenticity of Yanaka’s shitamachi (downtown) culture—a living museum of Tokyo’s pre-war history.

Once a sanctuary for artists and everyday tradespeople, Yanaka was one of the few neighborhoods spared from air raids during WWII. Now, a walk through its winding backstreets feels like stepping into a gentler, slower Tokyo. The scent of sawdust drifts through the open doors of tiny workshops where time seems to pause.

Have you explored this 'Old Tokyo'? 🌸 Save this for your next visit and bring a piece of Japan’s timeless spirit home.
