## Caption
The air carries a faint hint of incense, mingling with the earthy aroma of aged wood and the whisper of freshly brewed tea. A narrow alley unfolds ahead, dappled with sunlight slipping between weathered rooftops. A soft clinking sound draws you closer — it's the subtle rhythm of a ceramic artisan, shaping clay in the window of a tiny shop.

Welcome to Yanaka, Tokyo's "shitamachi" (old town), where time seems to pause. Unlike the modernized face of sprawling districts, this neighborhood reflects the resilience of history, surviving the WWII air raids and retaining its Edo-era street grid. Around the corner, a paper shop promises treasures, while a blue noren curtain sways, revealing the secrets of a hidden teahouse.
