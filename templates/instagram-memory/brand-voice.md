## Format constraints (hard)

- **Caption length:** 80–180 words.
- **Emojis in caption body: ≤2 total.** Hashtags don't count.
- **Hashtags: 3–6 total**, specific niche tags plus 1–2 account/location tags. No mega tags with millions of posts — small accounts are invisible in them.
- **No hashtags in the caption body.** Put them after a `.\n.\n.\n` separator at the end.
- **No links in the caption.**
